# Controls, electrical design and Belgian playback checks

Regulatory/source research dated 8 October 2026; allocation updated 9 October for the approved facade edition. Current design: 800 bullet pixels + 154 grouped strip addresses, with ground-floor window and door frame mounting. Final cable lengths and purchased specifications require commissioning checks. Regulatory sections below remain the dated research, not a newly verified legal opinion.

## Control recommendation

The final project uses three Baldrick8 Ethernet controllers, paired with retained 320.4 W supplies and A=368 / B=340 / C=246 RGB addresses, with xLights sequencing and one FPP player. [EU sourcing research](eu-sourcing-research.md) provides merchant evidence for this choice. WLED remains an alternative with the documentation below; its settings and limits do not automatically apply to Baldrick firmware.

Verified primary documentation: [xLights Ethernet controllers](https://manual.xlights.org/xlights/chapters/chapter-four-set-up/lighting-networks/ethernet-controller) supports DDP/WLED and FPP controllers. [WLED FAQ](https://kno.wled.ge/basics/faq/) recommends Ethernet and DDP for large realtime shows. [WLED multi-output documentation](https://kno.wled.ge/features/multi-strip/) recommends ESP32 and up to 512 pixels per pin on four outputs for its best documented performance tier. The proposed sub-300-pixel ports are comfortably within that guidance; this is not a guarantee of a particular firmware/build's frame rate.

[FPP project documentation](https://github.com/FalconChristmas/fpp) supports Raspberry Pi and Beagle platforms and E1.31/DDP output. Use a currently supported production image for a Raspberry Pi 3/4/5, reliable microSD, its proper mains adapter, wired Ethernet and USB audio adapter/DAC (especially Pi 5, which has no analogue audio jack). FPP host, audio hardware and network switch can stay indoors. [xLights FPP Connect](https://manual.xlights.org/xlights/chapters/chapter-five-menus/tools/fpp-connect) uploads FSEQ sequences, associated media and network-output definitions; select Media and UDP Outputs=All for this single-player design. Use the same master audio file while sequencing and playing.

DDP is the preferred protocol. [WLED DDP specification](https://kno.wled.ge/interfaces/ddp/) uses UDP port 4048 and does not read optional packet timecodes. Consequently the central FPP player sends frames in time with its own audio; these WLED nodes are realtime receivers, not FPP Multisync remote players. WLED built-in sound-reactive effects will approximate beats but cannot reproduce a predetermined song sequence. For a true clone, sequence effects against the exact legally acquired recording and manually confirm timing.

Set a 25 ms sequence interval (40 fps), reserve fixed IPs, disable autonomous effect/sync senders during playback, and test full-show motion with the final firmware before deployment. Retain exported configuration backups. Do not upgrade firmware during the show season without rerunning the acceptance test.

### Final channel and port allocation

Use the authoritative 19-prop, three-bank map in [the project](christmas-show-project.md) and `pixel-map.csv`: A channels 1–1104, B 1105–2124, C 2125–2862, receiving 368 / 340 / 246 local addresses. Do not use an earlier two-controller illustrative map. For WLED replacements, local LED indexes are zero-based while global xLights channels are one-based; map each DDP output's global source range into receiver offset zero. The project's separated E1.31 universes apply to the selected show controllers; WLED listening range must additionally be explicitly constrained or separated as its documentation advises.

## Power and outdoor construction — engineering recommendations

Use independently fused 12 V DC feeds for each pixel section. The label WS2811 alone does not establish maximum current: resistor and regulated 12 V nodes differ. Obtain the supplier's full-white current specification and measure a sample string. Pending that measurement, conservative design assumption is 0.06 A/pixel: 800 bullet pixels plus 154 strip groups can demand 57.24 A / 686.88 W at 12 V, conditional on grouped strip at <=7.2 W/m and 10 addresses/m. A 30% brightness sequence may reduce normal use, but software brightness/current limits are not cable or fire protection. Size supplies, fuses, connectors and distribution for the fault and maximum-load conditions; keep a real hardware safety margin.

Current bank maxima: A=22.08 A / 264.96 W, B=20.40 A / 244.80 W, C=14.76 A / 177.12 W. Three Mean Well RSP-320-12 supplies total 961.2 W nameplate output; verify thermal derating and controller overhead. Dedicated fused injection distribution avoids assuming controller output fuses can supply entire 100-node props or long strips. Verify board bank and terminal ratings with the merchant. WLED alternatives likewise require compatible power distribution: the [Dig-Quad guidance](https://quinled.info/quinled-dig-quad-diy-v3-specifications/) distinguishes board current limits from separately fused injection.

An RSP-320 is a building-in PSU, not weatherproof. Keep it dry with protective earth/touch protection and adequate ventilation, or have a competent assembler supply a complete documented outdoor enclosure. The project favours dry indoor/garage banks if cable routes permit.

Current schedule has 27 feeds: twelve 50-node sections (3 A, provisional 4 A fuse), ten 20-node poles (1.2 A, provisional 1.5 A fuse), and five strip sections up to 32 groups (1.92 A, provisional 2.5 A fuse). All selections remain subject to actual cable/connector ratings and fuse curves; see power-feeds.csv. Facade cables rise on the right and cross the entrance above the lintel at a modeled 2.55 m. No cable crosses the door threshold at paving level. Use injection at each section from the same PSU; cut/isolate the positive rail at boundaries fed by different PSUs. Never parallel independent PSU positive outputs without explicit manufacturer support. Maintain data ground reference as required by the controller/receiver; do not add casual DC-negative-to-earth bonds to a SELV design. Fuse every positive injection feed close to its power source and ensure a feed cannot be back-powered by an unfused adjacent positive rail. Both-end injection should share the same protected branch; separately sourced leads each require protection and a backfeed assessment.

Calculate voltage drop from actual length, copper area and full-white branch current: approximate copper round-trip R = 2 × 0.0175 × one-way metres / mm². Example: 5 m, 1.5 mm², 3 A drops about 0.35 V in the feed, before pixel wiring/connectors; 10 m at 6 A drops about 1.4 V. This calculation does not establish thermal ampacity. Put local distribution near props and avoid long high-current trunk leads. Check last-node voltage and colour on full white. Never raise PSU voltage above the pixel/controller rating to hide drop.

Use outdoor UV/cold-rated cable, sealed matching pigtails with verified pinout, proper cable glands and capped unused sockets. Aim for IP65/66 service boxes and IP67 mated pixel connectors, kept raised above runoff with drip loops and no pooling. A sealed box must also manage heat and condensation; use rated pressure-equalizing vents where the enclosure maker permits them. Keep mains and DC in segregated compartments; do not hot-plug strings. Keep bare data leads short; if distances cause flicker, use the controller maker's differential sender/receiver system rather than an arbitrary long GPIO wire. CAT5/6 is for Ethernet/differential signalling, not a substitute for the calculated DC power cable.

Use a verified earthed outdoor supply circuit with ≤30 mA RCD protection and overcurrent protection. Have an electrician verify actual protection, earthing, circuit sharing and whether any proposed fixed additions require inspection. Use outdoor rated, fully unwound extension leads and weatherproof connection boxes, one accessible isolator, no daisy chains or plugs lying in the garden. Route cables outside pedestrian/vehicle paths; use cable ramps where unavoidable. Stake/ballast props inside the property with anchors that account for wind and underground services. Stop operation after water ingress, recurring trips, damaged cables or unsafe wind; resolve cause before restarting.

### Verified Belgian regulatory basis, distinct from recommendations

[FOD Economie domestic installation safety](https://economie.fgov.be/nl/themas/energie/bronnen-en-dragers-van-energie/elektriciteit/veiligheid-en-controle-van/veiligheid-van-huishoudelijke) requires compliance with AREI and owner maintenance/required inspections. [AREI Book 1, 2026](https://economie.fgov.be/sites/default/files/Files/Publications/files/AREI-Bijlage-Boek-1-installaties-op-laagspanning-en-op-zeer-lage-spanning-2026.pdf): section 4.2.4.3 adds high-sensitivity differential protection for specified domestic socket/lighting circuits, subject to existing-installation exceptions; 7.4 expressly includes domestic outdoor installations and permits extra-low safety voltage within its limits. Table 5.4 links water exposure AD4/AD5/AD7 to IPX4/IPX5/IPX7 respectively. Thus an outdoor installation needs an exposure assessment; this is not a universal statutory IP65 rule. The 30 mA, H07RN-F and enclosure choices above are design recommendations to be checked for this house, not a claim that every old Belgian socket must already have that protection. [FOD latest regulations page](https://economie.fgov.be/nl/publicaties/algemeen-reglement-op-de) also lists a November 2026 revision affecting inspection seals, which should be checked at commissioning.

## Music, visitors and radio

[Unisono's family/private-event guidance](https://www.unisono.be/nl/knowledgebase/heb-ik-een-licentie-nodig-voor-mijn-prive-evenement-of-een-evenement-dat-ik-organiseer-in-familiekring) grants an exemption only when all its conditions hold: free and noncommercial without relevant artistic budget/sponsorship, no advertising, family-circle event, at home. A deliberately advertised street-facing show with an audience should not assume this exemption. Ask Unisono to classify a residential Christmas show, dates, audience area, recorded playlist and whether speakers or radio are used, then obtain the applicable licence/quote. Do not borrow a festival/sports tariff for this project. [Unisono conditions](https://www.unisono.be/sites/default/files/unisono_mib_nl.pdf) describe a surcharge for late notification under five days and event playlist reporting within 15 days. Allow several weeks for classification and confirmation.

Acquire the exact recording lawfully as a usable local audio file; a YouTube link or streaming subscription is not a project audio asset or public-use licence. Public playback clearance is distinct from filming/uploading a show. [Sabam creator guidance](https://www.sabam.be/nl/klanten-gebruikers/licenties/content-creators) explains that synchronization with video requires publisher and record-label permission, while some platform agreements may cover performance rights. For sharing a show video, check both composition and recording rights; alternatively use original commissioned music or a library track whose explicit terms cover public playback and synchronized published video. Public-domain composition does not automatically free a modern recording.

Avoid US-style garden FM transmitters as a default Belgian solution. [BIPT radio interface B11](https://www.bipt.be/file/cc73d96153bbd5448a56f19d925d05b1379c7f21/33f41557d82019ffc913e797d2ea2cebde9fac4b/25-11-b-nl.pdf) permits this short-range analogue FM category in 87.5–108 MHz only at 50 nW ERP with occupied bandwidth ≤200 kHz. Typical 0.1 W, 0.5 W or 1 W Christmas transmitters vastly exceed that limit. Do not order one unless BIPT confirms a lawful route for the proposed operation. Recommended first season: restrained local speakers during agreed early-evening hours, or silent light-only viewing with properly licensed accessible audio arrangements. Radio compliance would not replace music licensing. Ask the municipality about local sound limits, public-footpath use, traffic and event rules before inviting crowds; no municipality-specific requirement was verified here.
